Compassionate, full-property estate cleanouts

Clearing out a home is rarely just a chore. Whether you're grieving a loved one, helping a parent downsize, settling an estate as an executor, or preparing a property for sale, it can feel overwhelming, and there's usually a deadline attached. Albany Junk Removal takes the heavy lifting off your shoulders, handling everything from a single room to an entire house, garage, shed, and yard, so you can focus on the things that matter.

We move at your pace and treat every home with respect. Point out anything the family wants to keep (photos, documents, heirlooms, specific furniture), and we'll set it aside and leave it untouched. Everything else, we sort, carry out, and haul away, then leave the property clean and ready for the next step.

What an estate cleanout includes

We clear

  • Furniture, mattresses & appliances
  • General household goods & clutter
  • Garages, sheds, attics & basements
  • Yard items & outdoor clutter
  • The whole home, room by room

We set aside

  • Keepsakes & family heirlooms
  • Important documents & photos
  • Anything you mark to keep

Just walk us through it, or leave a list, and we'll double-check before anything leaves.

How estate cleanout pricing works

We price estate cleanouts by volume (how much of our truck the removed items fill), plus any special items that take extra work, like appliances, a piano, or a hot tub. After a quick walkthrough (in person or by photos and video), you get one honest, upfront price for the whole job. You approve it before we begin, and there are no hidden fees or hourly surprises. For larger estates, we can stage the work over more than one visit if that's easier.

Donation & recycling first

A lifetime of belongings deserves better than a dumpster. Wherever we can, we route usable furniture, housewares, and goods to local donation partners, and we recycle metal, appliances, and other materials. It's better for the community and the Mid-Willamette Valley, and many families find real comfort in knowing their loved one's things went to help someone else.

Built for families, executors & realtors

We regularly coordinate with executors and probate attorneys, out-of-town family members, and realtors who need a property cleared and photo-ready before listing. We can send photos and progress updates, work around closing and listing deadlines, and handle access details so you don't have to be on site the whole time.

Questions & answers

Estate cleanout FAQs

Common questions from families, executors, and realtors we work with.

How much does an estate cleanout cost in Albany, OR?
Estate cleanouts are priced by how much is removed, meaning the volume that fills our truck, plus any special items like appliances or a hot tub. You get an honest, upfront price after a walkthrough or a few photos, with no hidden fees. Call or reach out for a free quote.
Can you work with executors, realtors, and out-of-town family?
Yes. We regularly coordinate estate cleanouts for executors, probate attorneys, realtors preparing a listing, and family members who live out of the area. We can send photos and updates and work to your closing or listing deadline.
Do you donate or keep certain items aside?
Absolutely. Tell us what to set aside, such as keepsakes, documents, or anything the family wants to keep, and we will leave those untouched. We donate usable furniture and household goods and recycle what we can before anything goes to the landfill.
How quickly can you complete an estate cleanout?
Most homes can be cleared within a day or two of scheduling, and we can often start within a few days of your call. For larger estates we can stage the work over more than one visit to fit your timeline.
Do I need to be there the whole time?
No. Many families and executors are out of the area, so we can coordinate access, work from a list of what to keep, and send photos and progress updates so you do not have to be on site.
What happens to everything you remove?
We donate and recycle first. Usable furniture and household goods go to local donation partners, metal and appliances are recycled, and only what is left over is disposed of at the proper facility.
