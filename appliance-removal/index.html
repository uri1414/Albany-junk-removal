Full-service appliance haul-away

Appliances are heavy, and many contain metal and refrigerants that need proper handling. Our crew disconnects standard plug-in units, carries them out from the kitchen, laundry, garage, or basement, and hauls them off, so you do not have to wrestle a fridge down the stairs.

We remove refrigerators, freezers, stoves and ranges, ovens, dishwashers, microwaves, water heaters, air conditioners, and dehumidifiers, for homes, rentals, and businesses across Albany and the Mid-Willamette Valley.

How appliance removal pricing works

Appliance removal is priced by how much space the items take in our truck, plus a small allowance for heavier units like refrigerators that need extra handling. You get an honest, upfront price before we begin, with no hidden fees.

Responsible disposal & recycling

Appliances are mostly metal, so we route them to scrap-metal recycling, and refrigerators and freezers are handled responsibly so their refrigerant is recovered properly.

Questions & answers

Appliance removal FAQs

Common questions about appliance and refrigerator removal.

How much does appliance removal cost in Albany, OR?
It depends on the size and number of appliances and how far they have to be carried. Heavier units like refrigerators take a bit more. You get an upfront price before any work starts.
Do you take refrigerators and freezers?
Yes. We remove fridges and freezers and get them to facilities that recover the refrigerant and recycle the metal properly.
Do you disconnect the appliance?
We disconnect standard plug-in and standard water-line appliances. Gas lines or hard-wired connections should be capped by a licensed pro first, and we will flag it if needed.
Do you recycle old appliances?
Yes. Appliances are mostly metal, so we route them to scrap-metal recyclers rather than the landfill.
Can you remove a washer and dryer too?
Yes. We remove washers, dryers, and stacked units from laundry rooms, closets, basements, and upper floors.
Do you offer same-day appliance pickup?
Often, yes. Reach out early and we will try to get you scheduled the same or next day.
