How yard waste pricing works

Yard waste is priced by how much fills our truck, so a small pile costs less than a full landscaping haul. You get an upfront price before we load, with no hidden fees.

Responsible disposal & recycling

Green waste can usually be composted or recycled rather than landfilled, so we take it to yard-debris recycling wherever that is available.

Questions & answers

Yard waste removal FAQs

Common questions about yard debris and storm cleanup.

How much does yard waste removal cost in Albany, OR?
It is based on how much fills our truck. A small pile is inexpensive; a full yard overhaul is more. You get an upfront price before we load.
Do you load it, or do I have to pile it up?
We do the loading. You can pile it if you want, but you do not have to. We will gather it from where it is.
Do you remove small stumps?
Yes, we take small stumps and root balls. For large stumps, ask us and we will let you know what we can handle.
Do you compost or recycle the yard waste?
Whenever a yard-debris recycling or compost site is available, yes, rather than sending it to the landfill.
Can you help after a storm?
Yes. Storm cleanup is one of our common jobs, and we try to offer same-day or next-day pickup when the weather causes a mess.
Do you take old fencing and planters too?
Yes, along with the green debris we can remove old fence panels, planters, and similar yard items.
