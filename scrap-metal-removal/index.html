Scrap metal picked up and recycled

Metal is heavy and awkward to haul, but it is also one of the easiest things to recycle. We collect scrap metal from your home, garage, yard, or business and take it straight to the recyclers.

We remove old appliances, water heaters, metal furniture and shelving, fencing and pipe, BBQ grills, auto parts, and general scrap, for homeowners, landlords, and businesses across the Albany area.

How scrap metal removal pricing works

Scrap metal removal is priced by how much space it takes in our truck and the labor to move it. Please note we are a removal and recycling service, not a scrap buyer, so we haul it for you rather than pay per pound. You get an upfront price first, with no hidden fees.

Responsible disposal & recycling

Everything metal we collect goes to recyclers to be reclaimed, which is better for the environment than the landfill and part of how we work.

Our simple process

Tell us what metal you have and where it is. We confirm an upfront price and a time, then carry it out and haul it to the recycler. We can combine metal with a larger junk removal or cleanout in the same trip.

Scrap metal removal FAQs

Common questions about scrap metal pickup and recycling.

How much does scrap metal removal cost in Albany, OR?
It is based on truck space and the labor to move it. You get an upfront price before we haul, with no hidden fees.
Do you pay for scrap metal?
No. We are a removal and recycling service rather than a scrap buyer, so we haul it away for you and recycle it, rather than paying per pound.
What kinds of metal do you take?
Appliances, water heaters, metal furniture and shelving, fencing, pipe, grills, and general scrap. If you are not sure, just ask.
Do you do the heavy lifting?
Yes. Metal is heavy, and our crew carries it out from the garage, yard, or wherever it is.
Can you recycle it responsibly?
Yes. All the metal we collect goes to recyclers to be reclaimed instead of landfilled.
Can you take metal along with other junk?
Yes. We can combine scrap metal with a larger junk removal or cleanout in the same visit.
