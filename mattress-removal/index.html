Easy mattress removal and recycling

Mattresses are awkward to move, and many disposal sites charge special fees or turn them away. We take care of the whole thing. Our crew carries your old mattress and box spring out from any bedroom, apartment, or upper floor and hauls it off for good.

We remove every size, from crib mattresses and twins up to California kings, along with box springs, bed frames, futons, and adjustable bases, for homes, rentals, and property managers across the Albany area.

How mattress removal pricing works

Mattress removal is priced by how much room the items take in our truck, so a single mattress is quick and affordable, and multiple pieces cost a bit more. You get an upfront price before we begin, with no hidden fees.

Responsible disposal & recycling

Mattresses can often be recycled into steel, foam, and fiber instead of going to the landfill. We route them to recycling wherever it is available and dispose of the rest responsibly.

Our simple process

Send the form or call with the mattress size and where it is. We confirm an upfront price and a time, then carry it out and haul it away. Same-day and next-day pickups are usually available.

Questions & answers

Mattress removal FAQs

Common questions about mattress pickup in the Albany area.

How much does it cost to remove a mattress in Albany, OR?
A single mattress and box spring is one of our most affordable pickups. Pricing is based on truck space, so more pieces cost a little more. You always get an upfront price first.
Do you recycle old mattresses?
Yes, whenever recycling is available in the area. Mattresses contain steel, foam, and fiber that can be reclaimed instead of landfilled.
Can you take just one mattress?
Absolutely. No job is too small, and single-mattress pickups are common for us.
Do I need to carry it outside first?
No. We remove it from the bedroom or wherever it is, including upstairs and in apartments.
Can you remove a mattress when my new one is delivered?
Yes. Schedule us around your delivery and we will haul the old one away so you are not left with it.
Do you work with landlords and property managers?
Yes. We handle turnovers and multiple units, and can coordinate access and billing.
