Questions & answers

Junk removal FAQs

How much does junk removal cost in Albany, Oregon?
Pricing is based on how much space your items take up in our truck, so you only pay for what you actually throw out. You'll get an honest, upfront price before we start any work, with no hidden fees and no surprises. Request a free quote by phone, text, or through our online form.
What items do you take?
We remove almost anything, including furniture, mattresses, appliances, refrigerators, washers and dryers, hot tubs, scrap metal, construction debris, yard waste, and general household junk. We also handle full garage, storage unit, estate, and office cleanouts. No job is too big or too small.
Do you offer same-day junk removal?
Yes. We often offer same or next-day junk removal in Albany and the surrounding Mid-Willamette Valley. Call or request a quote and we'll get you scheduled quickly.
Do I need to move my items outside first?
No. Our friendly, professional crew does all the heavy lifting and loading. Just point to what needs to go, and we carry it out from anywhere on your property, then sweep up and leave the space cleaner than we found it.
What do you do with the junk you haul away?
We dispose of everything responsibly. Whenever possible we donate usable items and recycle metal, appliances, and other materials to keep them out of the landfill.
What areas do you serve?
We serve Albany, Oregon and the surrounding Mid-Willamette Valley, including Corvallis, Lebanon, Tangent, Millersburg, Jefferson, Sweet Home, Philomath, Brownsville, and the Salem area.
