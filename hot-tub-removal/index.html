Full-service hot tub & spa removal, start to finish

A hot tub is one of the hardest things to get rid of on your own. It's heavy, it's awkward, it's often wired in and plumbed, and it usually sits behind a fence or up on a deck. Albany Junk Removal takes the entire job off your plate. We come to your property, drain any water still in the shell, disconnect the spa, cut and break it down into manageable pieces, and carry every part out — then clean up the area where it sat. You don't lift, drain, or disassemble anything.

We remove all kinds of spas: plug-and-play hot tubs, full in-ground and above-ground units, swim spas, inflatable tubs, and long-dead units that haven't run in years. Whether it's in a backyard, on a patio, or built into a raised deck, we've moved one like it before.

What's included

We handle

Good to know

  • Hard-wired 220V lines should be disconnected by a licensed electrician first
  • We'll flag any gas or permanent plumbing that needs a pro to cap

Not sure how yours is wired? Just ask — we'll tell you exactly what's needed before the appointment.

How hot tub removal pricing works

Every hot tub is a little different, so we price each job on three things: the size of the spa, how much disassembly it needs, and how hard it is to reach (a tub on flat ground by the driveway is simpler than one up on a second-story deck). Once we see photos or take a quick look on site, we give you a firm, upfront price. You approve it before any work starts — and that's the price you pay, with no hidden fees or surprise add-ons.

Responsible disposal & recycling

Old spas don't have to go straight to the landfill. We separate out the metal frame, pumps, heaters, and components for scrap-metal recycling, and dispose of the shell and cabinet responsibly at the proper facility. Keeping usable material out of the landfill is part of every job we do.

Our simple process

Call, text, or send the quote form with a couple of photos of your hot tub. We confirm an honest, upfront price and a time that works for you. On removal day, our insured crew handles the draining, disassembly, and haul-away, then leaves the space clean. Most hot tub removals are done in a single visit — often the same or next day.
