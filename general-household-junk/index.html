Household junk removal FAQs

Common questions about everyday junk removal in Albany.

How much does junk removal cost in Albany, OR?
Pricing is based on how much space your items take in our truck, so you only pay for what you throw out. You get an upfront price before any work, with no hidden fees.
Will you take just one item?
Yes. No job is too small. We pick up single items all the time, often the same or next day.
What can't you take?
We can't haul hazardous materials like paint, chemicals, solvents, or asbestos. We will gladly point you to the right disposal site for those. Almost everything else, we take.
Do I need to move it outside?
No. Our crew carries everything out from anywhere in your home, then tidies up.
Do you donate or recycle?
Yes. We donate usable goods and recycle what we can before anything goes to the landfill.
Do you offer same-day junk pickup?
Often, yes. Reach out early and we will do our best to fit you in the same day.
