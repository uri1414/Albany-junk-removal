Debris hauling for remodels and demo

Piles of drywall, lumber, and old fixtures slow a project down and take up space. We remove renovation and demolition debris for homeowners and contractors, loading it ourselves so your crew keeps working.

How construction debris pricing works

Construction debris is priced by volume, with heavier loads like tile and concrete factored in since weight matters. You get an upfront price before we haul, with no hidden fees, and contractors can set up repeat pickups.

Responsible disposal & recycling

We separate and recycle metal, wood, and cardboard where possible and dispose of the rest at the proper facility, which keeps material out of the landfill and helps on green-building jobs.

Our simple process

Questions & answers

Construction debris removal FAQs

Common questions from Albany homeowners and contractors.

How much does construction debris removal cost in Albany, OR?
It is based on the volume and weight of the debris. Heavier materials like tile and concrete factor in. You get an upfront price before we haul, with no hidden fees.
Do you work with contractors?
Yes. We schedule around active job sites and can set up repeat pickups so debris never piles up.
Do you take concrete, dirt, or roofing?
We take small amounts of concrete and most renovation debris. For large volumes of concrete or dirt, ask us and we will let you know what we can do.
Do I need to rent a dumpster?
No. We bring the truck and the crew, load everything by hand, and only charge for what we haul, so there is no dumpster to rent or have sitting in the driveway.
Can you remove debris from inside the house?
Yes. We carry it out from wherever the work is happening, not just the curb.
How fast can you come?
Often the same or next day, which keeps your project moving.
